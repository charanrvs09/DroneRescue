DRONERESCUE EMERGENCY RESPONSE PROTOCOL

Purpose:
Define a coordinated response process for industrial emergencies using autonomous drone situational awareness.

1. INCIDENT DETECTION
- Receive alerts from factory monitoring systems, security systems, workers, or other authorized sources.
- Record incident type, location, source, timestamp, and alert confidence.
- Do not assume that an initial alert represents the complete situation.

2. INCIDENT EVALUATION
- The Incident Agent evaluates whether drone verification is required.
- Low-confidence alerts should not automatically trigger drone dispatch.
- Supported emergency categories include fire, chemical leak, intrusion, accident, structural damage, and flooding.

3. FLEET RESPONSE
- The Fleet Agent evaluates available drones.
- Consider battery level, current status, location, and sensor capabilities.
- Select the most suitable drone for the incident.

4. SITUATIONAL AWARENESS
- The selected drone should capture aerial evidence from a safe observation position.
- RGB imagery can identify visible hazards and structural conditions.
- Thermal imagery can identify heat signatures that may not be visible in RGB imagery.

5. AI ANALYSIS
- Vision analysis should produce an incident classification, confidence score, severity estimate, and supporting evidence.
- Low-confidence observations should trigger additional observation or repositioning rather than immediate escalation.
- High-confidence emergency detections may trigger additional sensor verification.

6. DYNAMIC RESPONSE
- The Decision Agent evaluates new evidence after each observation.
- The system may continue monitoring, request another observation, perform additional sensor verification, or recommend mission replanning.
- New information should be incorporated before continuing the mission.

7. SAFETY CONTROL
- Every operational action must pass through the Safety Governor.
- Unsafe actions must be blocked.
- AI agents must never override deterministic safety constraints.

8. HUMAN ESCALATION
- High-risk interventions require human authorization.
- The system should provide the operator with the incident, evidence, confidence, recommended action, and safety status.
- Human operators may approve, reject, or modify high-risk response actions.

9. MISSION COMPLETION
- Once the emergency assessment is complete, the drone should return to a safe location.
- Store mission results, sensor observations, decisions, and safety events for future analysis.

10. RESPONSE PRINCIPLE
Use AI to improve situational awareness and decision-making while keeping deterministic safety controls and human oversight in charge of high-risk operations.
