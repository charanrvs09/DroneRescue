DRONERESCUE BATTERY POLICY

Purpose:
Define battery-management rules for safe drone emergency operations.

1. MINIMUM OPERATING RESERVE
- A drone must maintain at least 25% battery to execute operational actions.
- Drones below 25% battery must not be dispatched.

2. FLEET SELECTION
- The Fleet Agent should consider battery level when selecting a drone.
- Prefer a drone with sufficient battery for the planned mission.
- Battery level alone must not override sensor or availability requirements.

3. MISSION MONITORING
- Battery status should be checked before critical actions.
- Long missions should account for the energy required to return home.
- A drone approaching a critical battery state should prioritize returning home.

4. LOW-BATTERY RESPONSE
- If battery falls below the configured safety threshold, new mission actions should be blocked.
- The system should recommend return-to-home or reassignment when appropriate.
- Another suitable drone may be selected if the mission still requires aerial support.

5. SAFETY GOVERNOR
- Battery constraints are enforced by the Safety Governor.
- AI agents must not override battery restrictions.
- A mission must never continue solely because an AI agent recommends continuing.

6. HUMAN OVERSIGHT
- Operators should be informed when battery conditions affect an emergency mission.
- Any exception to standard battery policy requires explicit human authorization and appropriate safety controls.

7. CORE PRINCIPLE
The drone must always retain enough operational margin to safely terminate its mission and return to a designated safe location.
