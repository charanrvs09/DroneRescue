DRONERESCUE SECURITY RESPONSE PROTOCOL

Purpose:
Define drone-assisted procedures for security incidents and unauthorized activity.

1. INCIDENT DETECTION
- Security alerts may originate from perimeter sensors, cameras, access-control systems, or authorized personnel.
- Record the incident type, location, timestamp, source, and confidence.
- The initial alert should be treated as evidence requiring verification.

2. DRONE DISPATCH
- The Incident Agent determines whether aerial verification is justified.
- The Fleet Agent should select an available drone with sufficient battery.
- Prefer a drone with appropriate RGB imaging capability for visual verification.

3. VISUAL VERIFICATION
- Capture RGB imagery from a safe observation position.
- Analyze imagery for people, vehicles, damaged barriers, unauthorized activity, or other relevant evidence.
- Low-confidence observations should trigger additional observation rather than immediate escalation.

4. OPERATIONAL SAFETY
- Every drone action must pass through the Safety Governor.
- The drone must remain within approved operational boundaries.
- The system must not intentionally endanger people or property.

5. HUMAN OVERSIGHT
- Identification of suspicious activity should be treated as an assessment, not automatic proof of wrongdoing.
- High-risk interventions require human authorization.
- AI agents must not independently initiate physical confrontation or enforcement actions.

6. DYNAMIC RESPONSE
- New observations should update the incident assessment.
- The Decision Agent may recommend continued monitoring, repositioning, additional image capture, or escalation to a human operator.
- If the situation changes significantly, the mission should be replanned.

7. EVIDENCE HANDLING
- Store relevant imagery, timestamps, drone identifiers, locations, analysis results, and confidence scores.
- Evidence should remain traceable to the observation that produced it.

8. MISSION COMPLETION
- After verification is complete, the drone should return to a safe location unless an authorized operator directs otherwise.

9. CORE PRINCIPLE
AI assists with security situational awareness, but human operators retain authority over consequential security decisions.
